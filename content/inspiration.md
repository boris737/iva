# What Iva's references tell us

18 images in Drive "iva 2 inspiration desines" (saved to `source/inspiration/`, git-ignored).
**Reference only. Never published.** They're other designers' work, three carry watermarks
(Antonovych, Namaq, a Chinese stock site), and several look AI-generated. Shown on her site,
they would read as her work.

## What she's drawn to
- **Materials:** walnut, board-marked concrete, travertine, brushed bronze/brass, olive lime plaster, raw stone.
- **Light:** low, warm, late afternoon; lamps glowing in dark rooms.
- **Forms:** rounded furniture, arches and curved walls, repeated panels, tall shelving.
- **Overall:** dark and warm, never stark white. 16 of 18 are interiors.
- **Two website screenshots** (Aura Studio, Kanvas): warm, serif, calm. She likes this register.
- **Palette measured from the 16 photos:** #1C130D, #342D22, #523524, #6A4D38, #8A6F58, #9E8F7D, #BAAD9B, #DAD1C5.

## How we use it
- The palette and typography for Direction C come straight from these measurements.
- Her own renders are golden-hour, which matches this mood. A dark warm frame makes them glow.
- Generated media is limited to **material textures** (walnut, travertine, concrete, plaster). No rooms, no buildings.
- Her interior work is the missing piece: 16/18 references are interiors, yet her folder has none.
